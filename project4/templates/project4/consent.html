Movie Preference Study

What this study is about

We are studying how to quickly learn a new user's movie taste. You will complete two short tasks: choosing between pairs of movies, and ranking short lists of movies from most to least appealing. There are no right or wrong answers — we are only interested in your personal preferences.

What you'll do

  • A brief pairwise-choice task and a brief ranking task (order randomized), each with on-screen instructions.
  • A handful of quick final comparisons.
  • A short questionnaire about your experience.
  • Total time: about 8–10 minutes.

Your privacy

We do not collect your name, email, or any other identifying information. Your browser is assigned an anonymous session identifier used only to link your answers together for analysis. Participation is entirely voluntary: you may close this page at any time without penalty, and there is no way for us to link an abandoned session back to you.
